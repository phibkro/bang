history chain (git · CHANGELOG · the PATH own that; growing it here is the *history-in-a-status-doc* smell —
run `/doc-smells`). Do NOT rewrite for routine commits or in-path progress — those belong in the active
`PATH-*.md`. A `@ <sha>` marks a stable conceptual waypoint (where a milestone settled), NOT live HEAD.
