=== WHY NO GREEN SLICE (the atomicity wall) ===
The evalD result tuple gains a 5th component (× CStore). This breaks EVERY consumer that
destructures evalD's output — 103 errors in AbstractMachine alone (sim/run_evalD binders,
tuple patterns, type annotations), + Wasm.lean (134 store mentions) + U5bComplete.lean (219).
The custom arm-def and the NoCustomFrame premise-drop are COUPLED: run_evalD's
`handle (.custom) M` case needs the term IH at NoCustomFrame (custom-frame :: K') = FALSE
while the premise is kept, so making the arm real makes that case unprovable-with-premise;
dropping NoCustomFrame is what makes the custom-frame IH available. They land together.
⟹ no intermediate compiles; the whole re-thread is one atomic commit.
